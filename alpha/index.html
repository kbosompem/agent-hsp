Demo checklist for tomorrow
  1. Open this file in Chrome or Edge on the laptop. First run needs internet once to fetch the Whisper model (~40 to 80 MB); it is cached after that. All processing is local.
  2. Click Open output window, drag that window to the projector or TV, then click FULLSCREEN (top right of that window) or double-click it. F11 also works.
  3. Pick the input. For the desk feed choose X-USB / XR16 (wiring below). Otherwise the built-in laptop mic works for a demo.
  4. Click Load model and wait for the green ready chip.
  5. Click Self test (no mic). It must print the John 3:16 sentence and pop a verse card. If this fails, the problem is the model, not your microphone.
  6. Click Test input and speak for ten seconds. It reports the input level and what it heard. If levels are low, untick raw desk feed (that lets the browser boost the mic) or pick another input.
  7. Click Listen. Say: “turn with me to John chapter three verse sixteen”. Say “there is no god” to see quote matching (Psalm 14:1). Say “Philippians chapter one verses one through four”, then “next verse” to advance the range.
  8. Say: “it is time for praise and worship.” A cue card appears. Press Enter to approve: 12-second splash, then “Amazing Grace” lyrics. Optional: load any MP3 first with Choose track and it will play under the lyrics.
  9. If the mic, model, or internet fails: press D. The scripted demo runs the entire flow with no microphone at all.
  10. Safety keys: Enter approve, Backspace skip, H hold all AI actions, B blackout, Space/←→ slide control (or say “next verse”), Shift+Esc panic (clears everything and blacks out).

XR16 wiring (one-time, on the mixer)

  • USB-B port on the back of the XR16 to the laptop. It appears as a 16-in / 4-out device, no driver needed.
  • X-Edit or the console: Routing → Card. Set Card Out 1 to a spare Mixbus (say Mixbus 6, named “Transcript”).
  • Send the pulpit mic post-fader at unity plus a little worship leader to that bus, out of the main mix path.
  • Peaks around -18 to -12 dBFS. Then select that device here as the input.
